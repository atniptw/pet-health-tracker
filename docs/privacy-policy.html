Privacy Policy — Pet Health Tracker

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Pet Health Tracker ("the app") is developed by Pootz&Boogie as a hobby project. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

To use the app, you sign in with a Google (or, in the future, Apple) account. We collect and store:

  • Account information from your sign-in provider: your name, email address, profile photo, and a unique account ID.
  • Household information you create or join: household name and membership.
  • Pet profiles you add: name, species, breed, birth date, and sex.
  • Symptom logs you record: symptom type, timestamps, notes, and any details you enter about the symptom.
  • Medication lists you add for a pet: medication name and notes.

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. A crash report contains the error and where in the app it happened, your device model, operating system version, and app version, and a random ID for this installation of the app. It does not contain your account information, household, pets, or logs.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos outside your sign-in profile picture, or any other data beyond what you enter into the app.

How your information is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's core function: letting you and the other members of your household log and view a shared history of your pets' symptoms. We do not use your data for advertising, do not run analytics or tracking of app usage, and do not sell or share your data with third parties, other than the infrastructure providers described below.

Sharing within a household

Pet Health Tracker is built for households, not single users. Anyone you invite into a household — or anyone who invites you into theirs — can see that household's pets, symptom logs, and medication lists, and can add their own symptom logs. Only household admins can add, edit, or remove pets and members. Keep this in mind before inviting someone or joining a household.

Where your data is stored

The app stores data using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), and sends crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics. Firebase is a service operated by Google. Firebase's handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Access to your household's data is restricted by server-side security rules to members of that household only.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account and household memberships exist. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at the address below and we will delete it manually. In-app account deletion is planned but not yet available.

Children's privacy

Pet Health Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
